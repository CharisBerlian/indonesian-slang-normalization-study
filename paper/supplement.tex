\section*{Representation-Change Diagnostics}
A post hoc vector-survival diagnostic found that, among text-changed test reviews, word TF-IDF vectors changed for 488/489 under the original-fitted PRDECT-ID vocabulary and 489/489 under the normalized-fitted vocabulary; vectors changed for 95/97 under either fixed SmSA word vectorizer. Under either fixed character vectorizer, they changed for 489/489 and 97/97, respectively. These counts show any vector change, not the fate of each replacement.
