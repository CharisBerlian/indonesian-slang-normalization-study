Pinned URLs and SHA-256 checksums are in `configs/resources.json` and `data/manifest.json`. `src/fetch_resources.py` retrieves only missing files, verifies known hashes, and does not download model weights. Previously generated private transformer payloads contain token IDs and labels that can potentially reconstruct text; they must not be treated as anonymous or included in a public package. Per-review predictions, gold labels, groups, split assignments and samples are also excluded from the snapshot pending review of applicable rights and privacy constraints.

The frozen source revision's README claims masked test labels in general; this study used the separately pinned labeled file recorded in `configs/resources.json` and its manifest. The distinction matters when obtaining the exact source and does not establish permission to redistribute it. None of the uncertain terms above is presented as evidence that prior use was unlawful.
